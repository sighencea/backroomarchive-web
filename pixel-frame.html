PIXEL//FRAME

Mechanical pixels

A mechanical pixel display made of magnetic flip tiles — no screen, no glass. Each tile flips on cue to render icons, messages, weather, calendar in a satisfying clack of physical motion. Screenless digital display, hypnotic by design.

The Ritual

Send. Flip. Read.

01

Send

Push from the companion app or any webhook. Weather, calendar, message, or a custom icon.

02

Flip

Tiles flip in sequence with that satisfying clack — the analog version of pixels turning on.

03

Read

The display holds the message until the next push. No glow, no screen burn, no battery drain.

Why it matters

Visually hypnotic, by design.

Mechanical clack

The sound is the feature. Tiles flip in waves; messages arrive with weight.

No-screen aesthetic

Zero glow, zero glare, zero blue light. A surface in the room — not a window into another one.

API-driven

Push from anywhere — app, webhook, IFTTT, Home Assistant. Build your own ambient display.

Wall-scalable

Single 16×16 frame, or chain frames into a 64×16 wall. Built to grow.

Premium build

CNC frame, custom-cast tiles, micro-servos rated for millions of flips.

Effortless install

Hang on the wall, plug in, scan the QR. The companion app does the rest.

Kickstarter-bound

The Pixel//Frame's demo video sells the campaign. Early backers get a numbered 16×16 frame; later tiers unlock larger grids and chained-frame wall kits. The most photogenic object in the catalogue.

The Ritual

Spin. Resist. Land.

01

Spin

Push the wheel with your thumb. Magnetic detents pulse with each step the encoder counts.

02

Resist

Programmable haptic resistance kicks in as you near the boundary — chapters, segments, scenes.

03

Land

Release. The deck snaps to the nearest cue. Press once to play, twice to mark.

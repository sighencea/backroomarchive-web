The Ritual

Pair. Press. Done.

01

Pair

BLE-pair to your phone or smart hub. The mono LCD shows the active profile, hub status, time.

02

Press

Each physical button is a macro — call mum, fire the focus scene, start the timer, lock the door.

03

Done

Action fires in milliseconds. No app, no scrolling, no notification storm. The button is the action.

Why it matters

Strong visual nostalgia, modern utility.

Physical buttons

Mechanical key feel under the thumb. Cheaper to repair, immune to gestures, satisfying to use.

No-screen attention

Mono LCD shows time, status, current profile. No feeds, no apps, no rabbit hole.

Smart-home hub

HomeKit, Home Assistant, IFTTT. The Mod//Phone is the physical button bank for the smart house.

Modular sides

Voice recorder, intercom, timer, alarm. Modules clip on the side and extend the hub.

Premium build

CNC aluminium shell, doubleshot keys, brass volume wheel. Built once, kept for years.

Long battery life

Mono LCD plus BLE means weeks per charge. Plug-in once a month, the rest just works.

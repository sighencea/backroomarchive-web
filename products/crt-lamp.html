The Ritual

Boot. Hum. Dim.

01

Boot

Tap the bezel. The lamp flickers, warms, settles into its phosphor glow over four seconds.

02

Hum

Gentle idle pulse — barely visible, instantly familiar. The room remembers something.

03

Dim

Tap again. The glow collapses to a centre dot, then fades out. CRT shutdown, faithfully.

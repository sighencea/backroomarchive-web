TAPE//LOCK

Secure by ritual

A physical encryption device shaped like a cassette. To unlock your files you must insert the tape into its dock and rotate it — a deliberate, analog ritual that replaces the password with an object you can hold. Hardware security with a hand on it.

The Ritual

Insert. Rotate. Access.

01

Insert

Slide the cassette into the dock. The deck reads its magnetic signature; the READY indicator wakes.

02

Rotate

Turn the cassette clockwise to its keyed position. Rotational sensors verify the precise angle and dwell.

03

Access

The vault decrypts on-host. Pull the tape and the volume re-encrypts in under a second.

Why it matters

Cybersecurity with a tactile ritual.

Offline first

Air-gapped by default. Your keys live on the cassette — never on a server, never in the cloud.

Physical key

Phishable passwords replaced by an object. If it isn't in the dock, the vault doesn't exist.

Ritual design

The slow gesture is the point. Friction creates focus. The ritual is what makes it stick.

USB-C dock

Plug in and forget the bezel. macOS, Windows, Linux. No drivers; standards-compliant HID.

Premium build

CNC aluminium, brushed black anodise, ceramic spindles. Built to outlast your laptop.

Different keys

Issue separate cassettes per vault — work, archive, family. Lose one, rotate the rest.

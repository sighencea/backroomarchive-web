Start the day, switched

A physical startup ritual device. Six toggle switches in sequence — flip them on to "boot" your day. Each one fires a routine: focus mode, lights up, calendar pulled, do-not-disturb on. Ritual beats productivity hacks.

The Ritual

Flip. Click. Begin.

01

Flip

Walk to the panel. Six switches, in order. Each one a deliberate physical commitment to the day.

02

Click

Lights, calendar, do-not-disturb, focus mode, music, timer. Each routine engages with its switch.

03

Begin

The day is booted. Reverse the sequence in the evening to power down — every step deliberate.

Why it matters

Ritual beats productivity hacks.

Tactile sequence

Six switches with audible click. The body remembers the order; the mind catches up later.

Linked routines

Each switch maps to a Shortcut, Webhook, or Home Assistant scene. Light up the day, literally.

Premium build

Walnut panel, aluminium frame, brass-base toggles. An object that earns its place on the wall.

Smart-home ready

HomeKit, Home Assistant, IFTTT, Webhooks. Plays well with whatever ecosystem you already run.

Family-friendly

The Ritual

Set. Spin. Click.

01

Set

Pick a profile from the companion app. The dial loads the right map for the active host.

02

Spin

Hook a finger and rotate. CNC metal under your hand, optical encoder counting every step.

03

Click

Each step lands with a mechanical click. Volume, zoom, scrub, jump — the feedback is the point.

Why it matters

Click-click-click, by design.

Mechanical click feedback

Real detent steps with audible click. The opposite of a touchpad — every move is felt.

Programmable mapping

Map any rotation to any input. Scrub, zoom, volume, layer, frame, channel — your call.

Premium build

CNC metal dial, weighted base, optical encoder rated for millions of cycles. Built once.

Cross-platform

macOS, Windows, Linux. Companion app handles per-app profiles via standard HID.

Profile per app

Auto-switch profile based on the foreground app. Photoshop, Logic, Premiere, VS Code, anything.

Wired or wireless

USB-C for zero-latency studio work. Bluetooth for the couch, the bench, the train.

The Dial//01 targets devs, designers, and editors who already invested in a good keyboard and chair. A peripheral that earns its desk space. Pro profile pack and engraving available at higher tiers.

The Ritual

Tap. Open. Restore.

01

Tap

Hold the vault to your phone. The NFC handshake authenticates and arms the magnetic latch.

02

Open

The latch releases with a soft click. Slide the shell open — the inner card lifts free.

03

Restore

Encrypted files mount on the host over USB-C. Close the shell and the volume re-locks instantly.

Why it matters

Memory you can hold, label and shelf.

Tap to unlock

NFC pairing replaces fumbled passwords with a physical gesture. Your phone is the key fob.

Memory you can hold

Files stored on a card you can label, slot into a sleeve and stack on a shelf — not scattered across servers.

Magnetic latch

Mechanical lock plus electronic verify — both must agree. The shell stays sealed until both pass.

USB-C fallback

Plug in for anything without NFC — macOS, Windows, Linux, iOS, Android. Standards-compliant, no drivers.

Premium build

CNC aluminium shell, laser-engraved labels, ceramic spindle. Built to be passed down, not thrown out.

Editions & engraving
